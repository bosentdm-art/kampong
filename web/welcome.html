How close does it get?

Every month we check our estimates against what flats actually sold for. Last year, half of our HDB estimates were within 2.7% of the final price. We'd rather show you where we miss than pretend we don't, so the full record is here.

The prices come from HDB's and URA's public sale records. No agent pays us, and there are no ads.
