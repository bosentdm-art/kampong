Kampong's link reader

If you've seen Kampong/1.0 in your site's logs, this is what it is.

Kampong is a home-price assistant for Singapore. When someone pastes a link to a property listing into Kampong, it reads that one page, once, at that person's request, the way a messaging app reads a link to show a preview. It compares the listing with recorded sales and tells the person whether the price is fair.

What it does and doesn't do

  • It reads only the page a person pasted. It doesn't follow links, search your site or fetch anything in the background.
  • It always says who it is (Kampong/1.0) and never pretends to be a normal browser.
  • If your site refuses (a block, a challenge page or a CAPTCHA), it stops and asks the person to paste the text instead. It doesn't retry or try to get around it.
  • It keeps only the listing's facts (price, address, size, floor, description), with names, phone numbers and emails removed.
  • It reads at most a few dozen links a day for any one person.

To stop Kampong reading your pages

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: Kampong
Disallow: /

Kampong checks for rules written for it by name before reading a page. You can also block Kampong/1.0 outright, or write to us and we'll stop reading your site.
